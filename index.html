OUR STORY

Started in a real garage.

We are computer engineers who traded ping pong tables for server racks.

What began as late nights debugging nodes on folding tables in a garage is now enterprise-grade validator infrastructure. No VCs, no buzzwords. Just engineers who care about uptime, security, and decentralization.

We monitor our own infra, we write our own tooling, and we answer our own pagers at 3AM. That's the garage way.
